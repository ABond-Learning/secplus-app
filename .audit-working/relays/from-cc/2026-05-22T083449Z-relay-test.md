## Round-trip success criteria

- CC's poll loop detects the new from-supervisor file within
  ~10s of the bridge-script push completing.
- CC processes it, writes a "test complete" ack to from-cc/
  referencing the supervisor's response filename.
- Total wall-clock for the relay legs (excluding supervisor's
  thinking time) under 30s.
