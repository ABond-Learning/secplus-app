## 2. Per-item resolution options

For each item in the pool, SB-fix-2 produces ONE of the following
decisions:

### 2.1 `re-cite-to-sybex`

Replace `messerVideo` + `subObjective` with a `sybexReference`
+ `comptiaObjective` pair (or extend the schema to hold both).
Item content stays unchanged. Use when:

- The concept is taught explicitly in Sybex
- The CompTIA SY0-701 objectives PDF lists the concept (or
  acronym) verbatim
- Messer's video is the wrong frame (catalogue should not
  pretend Messer covers this)

### 2.2 `rewrite-to-messer`

Rewrite the item's tested concept to match what Messer
actually teaches in the cited transcript. Use when:

- The umbrella concept is in the cited video but a DIFFERENT
  specific technique would also test the same teaching
- The CompTIA objective is broad enough that a rewrite is
  still on-objective
- Aiden would benefit from studying what Messer actually
  covers vs a corner-case technique not in his primary source

Trade-off: rewrites change SM-2 indices? No — same item position
just different content. But Aiden's history records the original
content; the rewrite invalidates prior attempts. Mitigation: log
the rewrite in `audit_d_review.sb_fix_2.original_content` so
audit can replay.

### 2.3 `keep-with-sybex-note`

No catalogue mutation. Add an `audit_d_review.sb_fix_2.note`
explaining that the item tests a Sybex-only concept and the
Messer citation is the closest umbrella. The validator stays
clean (citation is still valid per its rules); the audit trail
captures the secondary-source status for future review.

Use when:

- Sybex covers the concept; Messer's umbrella is acceptable
- Item is good as-is; no rewrite needed
- Lowest-cost option for partial-depth items where the
  umbrella relationship is intact

This is likely the default for most Pool A partial-depth items.

### 2.4 `flag-for-removal`

Mark the item for removal. Decision deferred to a later cleanup
pass (out of scope for SB-fix-2). Use when:

- Neither Messer nor Sybex covers it
- The concept is not on the CompTIA SY0-701 objectives PDF
- Cannot be plausibly tested using Aiden's primary sources

Likely small in number — most "wrong source" items can be
re-cited to Sybex; outright removal is reserved for items that
test material outside the exam scope.

### 2.5 `promote-to-sybex-citation`

(Variant of 2.1.) Add `sybexReference` + retain `messerVideo` as
an "indirect-reference" annotation. Schema extension: item can
have BOTH citations. Use when:

- Concept is in Sybex AS PRIMARY
- Messer's umbrella video is a useful adjacent reference
- The item is dual-cited going forward

Whether this lands as a new option or as a variant of `re-cite-to-sybex`
depends on Q-E (schema decision).

## 3. Sybex / source authority hierarchy

When supervisor decides where an item "really lives," the
hierarchy of authoritative sources:

| Tier | Source | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CompTIA SY0-701 Exam Objectives PDF | The concept or acronym appears verbatim in the objectives document. This is the canonical exam syllabus and beats all other sources. |
| 2 | Sybex SY0-701 Study Guide (Chapple, 9th edition — confirm with Aiden) | Concept has an explicit treatment in the study guide. Cite chapter + section or page. |
| 3 | Other major SY0-701 prep (Mike Meyers AIO, Pearson Cert Guide, Boson) | Used only if Sybex doesn't cover and Tier 1 is silent. Less common. |
| 4 | Industry-standard reference (NIST SPs, RFCs, vendor docs) | When the concept is real and exam-relevant but the prep books skip it. Common for protocol details (DNSSEC RFC, HSTS RFC). |
| 5 | Wikipedia / general technical reference | Last-resort grounding; rarely sufficient on its own for "this is exam material." |

**Decision rule:** an item with Tier 1 presence is keepable.
Without Tier 1 OR Tier 2 presence, the item is a removal
candidate (§2.4).

## 4. Two sub-paths

### 4.1 Sub-path P — partial-depth (40 items in Pool A)

**Default treatment:** `keep-with-sybex-note`. Cited Messer
video IS umbrella; specific technique is a Sybex-depth detail.
Catalogue stays as-is; audit field records that Sybex is the
primary source for the specific technique.

**Exceptions:**

- If Sybex actually doesn't cover the technique either AND it's
  not on the CompTIA objectives PDF → `flag-for-removal`.
- If the Messer umbrella video has a related sibling-technique
  that could replace this one → `rewrite-to-messer`.
- If the specific technique IS on the CompTIA objectives PDF
  but Messer's umbrella video isn't a fair representation →
  `re-cite-to-sybex`.

Expected distribution per CC's projection (subject to per-item
review): ~70% `keep-with-sybex-note`, ~15% `re-cite-to-sybex`,
~10% `rewrite-to-messer`, ~5% `flag-for-removal`.

### 4.2 Sub-path G — messer-curriculum-gap (2 items in Pool A)

**Default treatment:** deeper per-item decision. Cited Messer
video is a SIBLING concept (umbrella mismatch), not the umbrella.
Choices:

- `re-cite-to-sybex` if Sybex covers + CompTIA objective lists
- `rewrite-to-messer` if a related concept that IS taught in a
  Messer video can replace this item
- `flag-for-removal` if neither

Both current Pool A messer-curriculum-gap items (integer overflow
×2 in §2.3.2) need this deeper review. Per Report-#0007 §6,
integer overflow is a CompTIA SY0-701 objective term (it's in
the buffer-overflow umbrella per the objectives but Messer didn't
cover it). Likely outcome: `keep-with-sybex-note` with explicit
Sybex citation (because the CompTIA objective listing IS Tier-1
authority).

### 4.3 Sub-path R — orphan SB1.6 loose flags (Pool B, 18 items)

**Pre-step:** each item gets a subcategory decision (P, G, or
neither) before SB-fix-2 treatment.

This is similar to SB-fix-1a's TASK 1 cluster verification — CC
pre-greps each item's specific term against the cited transcript
+ corpus-wide; supervisor reviews the verdicts and assigns the
subcategory inline. After routing, items flow into Sub-path P
or G as normal.

Some Pool B items may turn out to be `category=out-of-source` but
NOT `sb16-candidate` (e.g. genuine catalogue bugs that should
have been re-cited in earlier sub-batches). Those would fold into
a residual D1/D3/D4/D5 partial-adjacent cleanup pass — separate
from SB-fix-2 but adjacent in scope.

Decision-specific fields:

- `decision="keep-with-sybex-note"`: as shown above.
- `decision="promote-to-sybex-citation"`: adds item-level `sybexReference` while retaining `messerVideo`.

Whether `sybexReference` lives at item-level or only inside
`audit_d_review.sb_fix_2` is Q-E.

## 9. Open questions for supervisor (Q-letters)

- **Q-A-2:** Skip Pool B entirely from SB-fix-2 scope; treat Pool B

  Pros: keeps SB-fix-2 narrowly focused on already-flagged items.
  Cons: 18 items linger in verdicts file without catalogue

- **Q-A-3:** Interleave — for each SB-fix-2 packet, include some

them indefinitely leaves the catalogue in an inconsistent state

### Q-C — Per-packet sizing for SB-fix-2

- **Q-C-1:** As proposed in §5 — 20 for P, 2 for G, 18 for R (routing).
- **Q-C-2:** Uniform 25-item packets regardless of sub-path. Reasoning:
  cadence Rule 1's first-packet-25 default applies; sub-path mixing in
  a packet is fine if supervisor adjudicates each item independently.
- **Q-C-3:** Smaller — 15-item packets across the board. SB-fix-2 is
  per-item judgment-heavy; smaller packets keep review tempo sustainable.

CC's lean: Q-C-1 (sub-path-tuned). Cadence Rule 1's "size matches
content complexity" reasoning argues for per-sub-path tuning over
uniform sizing.

### Q-D — Removal threshold — when does `flag-for-removal` fire?

- **Q-D-1:** Item tests a concept NOT in CompTIA objectives PDF
  AND NOT in Sybex/Tier-2 source → flag-for-removal.
- **Q-D-2:** Higher bar — also requires that no major SY0-701 prep
  resource covers it (Q-D-1 conditions PLUS no Tier-3 hits).
- **Q-D-3:** Lower bar — any item not in CompTIA objectives PDF
  is removal candidate, even if Sybex covers it.

CC's lean: Q-D-1. Items in Sybex are exam-relevant by definition
(it's the official prep guide); removing them under-fits the exam.

### Q-E — `sybexReference` schema location

- **Q-E-1:** Item-level field (`item.sybexReference = {edition, chapter, …}`).
  Pros: per-item citation surface for future UI/audit. Cons: needs
  validator update; schema extension on MatchItem / CramTerm / mc /
  scen.
- **Q-E-2:** Inside `audit_d_review.sb_fix_2` only. Pros: zero
  validator/schema impact; SB-fix-2 stays tooling-metadata. Cons:
  if future UI wants to display Sybex source on an item card, the
  data is nested deeper.
- **Q-E-3:** Both — item-level for `re-cite-to-sybex` /
  `promote-to-sybex-citation` decisions; audit-only for `keep-with-sybex-note`.

CC's lean: Q-E-2 for the SB-fix-2 first pass. Keeps the surface
narrow; defers the item-level decision until UI need is concrete.
Q-E-1 / Q-E-3 are clean future extensions.

### Q-F — Sequencing — partial-depth (P) vs messer-curriculum-gap (G) first?

- **Q-F-1:** P first (40 items, ~70% of pool, mostly mechanical
  `keep-with-sybex-note` defaults). Establishes the rhythm and
  Sybex-lookup workflow before tackling the harder G items.
- **Q-F-2:** G first (2 items, deeper decisions). Pros: clears the
  awkward ones; downstream mass is uniform partial-depth.
- **Q-F-3:** Bundle G into the first P packet (22 items combined).
  Pros: supervisor reviews both shapes in one pass.

CC's lean: Q-F-2 then P. Two messer-curriculum-gap items as a
calibration packet gets the Sybex-citation pattern + schema choice
exercised on the hard cases first. P then runs faster.

### Q-G — Routing pre-step for Pool B — methodology

Assuming Q-A-1 (routing first), how does the routing packet
work?

- **Q-G-1:** Re-use SB-fix-1b's cluster verification methodology —
  for each Pool B item, CC pre-greps the specific term against the
  cited transcript + cited Sybex (if Aiden provides a digital copy)
  + corpus-wide; supervisor reviews and assigns the subcategory.
- **Q-G-2:** Pure supervisor judgment, no pre-grep. CC just lists
  the 18 items with current cited video; supervisor decides.
- **Q-G-3:** Skip routing entirely — assume all 18 are
  `partial-depth` and proceed. Reasoning: SB1.6 already flagged
  them as candidates for partial-depth (`sb16_action="flag-for-review"`
  with `1 prose marker` met). Without the second marker the
  classifier didn't auto-flip but a loose flag was the closest
  call.

CC's lean: Q-G-1 — pre-grep is cheap and aligns with the cadence
Rule 2 pattern from SB-fix-1b. Adds calibration value early.
